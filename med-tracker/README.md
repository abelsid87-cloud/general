# 💛 Daily Medication Tracker

A simple, phone-friendly checklist to help track the Instituto Bernabeu
embryo-transfer medication plan (plus thyroid medication and
hydroxychloroquine) one day at a time.

## How to use it

1. Open **`index.html`** in any web browser (phone, tablet or computer).
2. You'll see today's medicines grouped into **Morning ☀️ / Midday 🌤️ / Evening 🌙**.
3. **Tap a medicine once you've taken it** — it turns green and gets ticked off.
4. The ring at the top shows how many are done for the day.
5. Use **‹ ›** to look at another day, or **↩ Back to today** to return.

Your ticks are saved automatically on your own phone (nothing is sent anywhere),
and each day starts fresh.

### Add it to your phone's home screen (so it feels like an app)

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen**.

## What it tracks

Based on the plan, the schedule changes by date and the app handles this automatically:

| Medicine | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Thyroid medication 50 mcg | Morning | Every day |
| Hydroxychloroquine 200 mg | Morning & Evening | Every day |
| Aspirin 100 mg | Morning | 1 pill |
| Prednisone 25 mg | Morning | From 9 Jun |
| Clexane 40 mg | Evening | Injection, from 9 Jun |
| Progynova 2 mg | Morning, Midday, Evening | From 11 Jun |
| Cyclogest 400 mg | Evening (11 Jun), then Morning & Evening | Vaginal capsule, twice daily from 12 Jun |

It also lists the upcoming **appointments** (progesterone blood test on 15 Jun,
and the embryo transfer on 16 Jun).

## ⚠️ Important

This is a **personal reminder tool only**. Always follow the exact written
instructions from Instituto Bernabeu and your doctors. **Never stop any
medication unless your clinic tells you to.** If anything in the app doesn't
match your prescription, trust your clinic — not this app.
