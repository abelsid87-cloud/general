Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Rare Beauty

At Pierres Mano, every jewel begins as a vision — a connection between the raw wonder of a gemstone and the hands of a master craftsman. Based in the heart of Casablanca, we blend Moroccan artistry with contemporary fine jewelry design.

Our gemologist-founders curate only the finest stones — diamonds, sapphires, emeralds and more — each selected for its exceptional quality and certified authenticity. Every piece is crafted to last a lifetime and beyond.

What We Offer

Our Services

From a single gemstone to a complete bespoke jewel — we accompany you through every stage of your jewelry journey.

Bespoke Creations

Work directly with our master jeweler to design a one-of-a-kind piece tailored to your vision, personality and budget.

Gemology Expertise

Our certified gemologists analyze, evaluate and certify precious stones. Professional appraisal for purchase, inheritance or insurance.

Jewelry Restoration

Breathe new life into heirloom pieces. We restore, resize, re-set stones and refinish precious metals to their original splendour.

Certified Gemstones

Purchase loose diamonds, sapphires, rubies and emeralds with full documentation. Sourced ethically and verified by our in-house gemologist.

Investment Advice

Precious stones as a store of value. Our experts guide you in selecting gemstones with strong appreciation potential.

Free Morocco Delivery

Enjoy complimentary insured delivery to any address across Morocco. Every parcel is discreetly packaged and fully tracked.
