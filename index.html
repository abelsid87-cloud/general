Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Rare Beauty

At Pierres Mano, every jewel begins as a vision — a connection between the raw wonder of a gemstone and the hands of a master craftsman. Based in the heart of Casablanca, we blend Moroccan artistry with contemporary fine jewelry design.

Our gemologist-founders curate only the finest stones — diamonds, sapphires, emeralds and more — each selected for its exceptional quality and certified authenticity. Every piece is crafted to last a lifetime and beyond.
